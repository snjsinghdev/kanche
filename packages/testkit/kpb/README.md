# KPB dataset

The Kanche Physics Benchmark's reality suite (KPB v1 test 15) scores the simulation against filmed marbles. The dataset format, the Tracker import, the fits and the scorer live in `tools/kpb`. How to film and fit is in [`docs/physics-calibration.md`](../../../docs/physics-calibration.md), and the scores are in [`docs/kpb.md`](../../../docs/kpb.md).

## What is here

`synthetic-v1.json` is **synthetic**. It was generated by `pnpm kpb synthetic` from the simulation itself, using known coefficients (`truth` in the file) that differ from v1. It has realistic scatter from repeat to repeat and tracking noise on every frame. It proves that the fitters recover the coefficients they were given and that the scorer tells right parameters from wrong ones. **It is no evidence about real marbles.** Its `synthetic` flag is `true`, and KPB's reality gate stays open until filmed shots replace it.

Filmed shots go in a new file, for example `filmed-v1.json`, added with `pnpm kpb import` and flagged `"synthetic": false`.

## Format (`kanche-kpb-dataset/1`)

A dataset is `{ schema, synthetic, description, truth?, recordings }`. Each recording is one filmed shot. It has these fields:

- `id`
- `condition`: repeats of one shot share it, and their spread is the real-world scatter
- `kind`: one of `roll`, `stun`, `head-on`, `cut`, `bank`, `hole` or `flick`
- `fps`: the rate the tracks are sampled at
- `court`: a physics `CourtSpec`
- `marbles`: their positions at the first frame
- `shooter` and `launch`: `rolling` off a ramp, or `stun`
- `vx` and `vy`: the shooter's measured launch velocity in m/s
- `fingerSpeed`: flicks only, in m/s
- `tracks`: one per tracked marble, `{ id, t0, x[], y[] }`, with positions in metres at `t0 + k / fps` seconds

`tools/kpb/src/dataset.ts` validates the format.
